📈 What is Trading?

Trading is the buying and selling of financial assets such as currencies, gold, stocks, and cryptocurrencies with the goal of making a profit.

💱 Forex Trading

The Forex market is the world's largest financial market, where currencies are traded 24 hours a day during the trading week.

🥇 Gold Trading

Gold is one of the most popular assets among traders because of its liquidity and strong price movements.

₿ Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum have become popular markets, but they are highly volatile and require careful risk management.

⚠ Risk Management

Never risk more than 1–2% of your trading capital on a single trade. Protecting your capital is more important than chasing profits.
